Ghosts

Act 2, Scene 1

[SCENE.—The same room. The mist still lies heavy over the landscape.]

MRS. ALVING. [Goes to the door.] Regine!

REGINE. [In a low voice from the dining-room.] What is it?

MRS. ALVING. Look in here. How is Mr. Manders getting on?

REGINE. He is coming upstairs now.

MRS. ALVING. Go in to him then.

REGINE. Must I, ma'am?

MRS. ALVING. Yes, go in. I want to speak to him alone.
